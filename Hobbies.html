My Hobbies

What I love to do in my free time are mainly:

  • -Coding
  • -Gaming with friends online or offline
  • -Watching shows and anime
  • -Listening to music

Coding

I gained interest in coding when I was in high school. I learned how games and other programs where made through coding and it piqued my interest. I had so much fun learning it even if the code did not work properly. The jankiness of coding is one of my favorites parts of it. I gain satisfaction once the code finally works properly.

Gaming

Gaming is my top 1 hobby when I get the time. It helps relax and socialize online with other people. I have been in a couple communities and the people I have met were fun and kind. It also help me gain connection to people with similar interest. Games I love are usually MMO's, RPG, Sandboxes, Platforming, Gacha games. Some examples of these games are: Minecraft, Umamusume: Pretty Derby, Where Winds Meet, and Wuthering Waves.

Shows and Anime

I fell in love with shows when I was a kid. It had entertained me and showed the fun and sad moments the characters have been through. I used to watch them with my family. I usually just watch anime now and its many genres like action, romcom, fantasy. My favorite animes would include Frieren: Beyond Journey's End, One Piece, Umamusume: Cinderella Gray. Theres many more I have in my favorites but I decided on these to be my top 3.

Music

Music has helped through tough times especially during the pandemic. I listen to music when I would want to focus on my tasks or when I feel down. I listen to songs that usually come from anime openings or ending credits but mostly it is j-pop. One artist that shone through me is Ado, a faceless singer with an amazing vocal. Even if I did not understand japanese the feelings put into their voices is what I understood.
